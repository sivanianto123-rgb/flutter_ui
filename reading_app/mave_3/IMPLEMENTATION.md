# Mave — What the App Does

Mave teaches toddlers (ages 0–2) to read by starting with sound. The core idea: before a child can read a word, they need to hear and feel its sounds. Every activity in the app builds this phonological awareness through listening, speaking, touching, and repeating.

---

## Getting Started

When a parent opens the app for the first time, they enter their child's name. The app then drops the child into the home screen, ready to learn.

---

## Home Screen

The home screen shows a simple learning path with three nodes:

- **Ma** — the first sound
- **Pa** — the second sound
- **Ma + Pa** — a combo, locked until the child has mastered both Ma and Pa

Each node shows how well the child is doing on that sound (as a percentage). Nodes pulse gently to invite tapping. Mastered nodes turn green. The combo node unlocks only when both individual sounds hit 90% accuracy.

A profile ring in the corner shows overall progress. Parents can tap it to see a detailed breakdown of their child's development.

---

## Sound Mode — The Core Learning Loop

Tapping a node starts a **Sound Mode session** — 10 sequential levels that teach one sound (e.g., "ma") through progressively interactive activities.

A dot bar at the top shows which level the child is on. A skip button lets them move ahead. A home button returns them to the home screen at any time.

### Level 1 — Listen & Watch

The syllable appears on screen, split into its letters (e.g., "M" + "A"). The app reads it aloud — first the whole syllable, then each letter separately — while the letters animate in sync. The child just watches and listens. If the microphone picks up any vocalization, the app celebrates with a star.

### Level 2 — Feed the Monster

Three animal cards appear. A word bubble at the bottom shows the target syllable. The child drags the bubble into the mouth of the correct animal. A correct drop turns the card green and plays a celebration sound. A wrong drop flashes red and resets. Three rounds total.

### Level 3 — Bubble Hunt

Nine bubbles are scattered across the screen — three are the target sound, six are distractors. The child taps bubbles to hear what they say. Tapping a target bubble pops it with an explosion animation and a celebration sound. If the child goes quiet for a few seconds, the target bubbles start breathing gently to draw attention. All three targets must be popped to complete the level.

### Level 4 — Sound Tracing

A bee appears on the left side of the screen. The child speaks or makes any sustained sound to move the bee rightward. The louder and longer they hold the sound, the further the bee travels. The goal: guide the bee all the way across the screen.

### Level 5 — Echo the Animal

An animal appears and the app says: "The [animal] says [sound]. Now you say it!" The microphone listens for 3 seconds. Any vocalization counts as a successful echo. Three animals, three rounds.

### Level 6 — Tap & Grow

A seedling sits on screen. The child speaks to make it grow into a sunflower. The longer they sustain the sound, the taller the flower grows. Full bloom = level complete.

### Level 7 — Who's Behind the Door?

Three doors appear. The app asks: "Who says [sound]?" One door hides something that starts with the target sound (e.g., a Monkey for "Ma"). The child taps doors to find it. A correct tap opens the door and plays a celebration. A wrong tap flashes red. Three rounds total.

### Level 8 — Phoneme Train

Three train wagons appear, each labeled with a syllable. The app says: "Tap the wagon that says [sound]!" The child taps the correct wagon, and the train chugs forward with a celebration sound. Wrong taps flash red. Three rounds.

### Level 9 — Storybook

A short story appears — one generated to feature the target sound many times throughout. The app reads the story aloud, sentence by sentence, with each sentence highlighted as it's narrated. Every instance of the target syllable is shown in a different color throughout the text.

### Level 10 — Word Reading

The child reads short words that contain the target sound (e.g., MA, MAP, MAT). Each word appears in large text with the target sound highlighted. The app reads the word first, then the microphone listens for any vocalization from the child. Four words per session.

---

## Accuracy & Unlocking

The app quietly tracks how the child is doing across all activities:

- **First-try correctness** on tap and drag games
- **Duration of sustained vocalizations** on voice games
- **Any vocalization at all** on listening and echo games

This rolling accuracy determines whether a sound node shows as "in progress," "mastered," or remains locked. Nodes unlock new challenges as a child gets better. The Ma+Pa combo node only opens when both individual sounds are at or above 90% accuracy.

---

## Parent Insights

Parents can tap the profile ring on the home screen to open a detailed view of their child's development across four areas:

- **Vocalization** — How clearly and consistently the child is producing sounds
- **Fine Motor** — Tap and drag accuracy
- **Cognitive** — Pattern recognition and attention during listening
- **Emotional** — Engagement and participation

Each area is shown as a percentage with a short description. This view is for parents only — the child's experience is never interrupted by data.

---

## What the App Does Not Do

- It does not require a stable internet connection. Audio is cached so the child can keep learning even offline.
- It does not require a parent to be present during a session. The entire flow is designed for a toddler to navigate independently.
- It does not rush the child. There are no timers or penalties. Nudges (gentle animations, audio prompts) encourage without pressuring.
